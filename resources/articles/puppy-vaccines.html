What Vaccines Does My Puppy Need?

Understand core puppy vaccines, timing, boosters, and lifestyle-based protection.

Educational note: This general information cannot diagnose a pet or replace an examination.

The short answer

Puppies need a carefully timed vaccine series because protection from maternal antibodies fades unpredictably during early life.

Why the details matter

Core protection commonly includes distemper, adenovirus, parvovirus, and rabies. Other vaccines depend on environment, travel, boarding, and exposure.

Your pet's age, history, environment, medications, and recent changes all affect what advice is appropriate. Keep notes about timing, frequency, appetite, activity, and anything that makes the concern better or worse.

When to contact a veterinarian

Call when a change is persistent, recurring, painful, worsening, or accompanied by weakness, breathing trouble, repeated vomiting, bleeding, collapse, or difficulty urinating. Emergency signs should not wait for an online response.

How we can help

A veterinary visit brings the history, physical examination, and—when useful—targeted testing together. The goal is not simply to name a problem, but to create a safe and realistic next step.
