Connected care

Here for the routine—and the unexpected.

From first vaccines to senior comfort, we bring your pet’s full health story into every decision.

Wellness & Preventive Care

Regular exams help us establish what healthy looks like for your pet and notice subtle changes early.

  • Comprehensive exams
  • Parasite screening
  • Nutrition guidance
  • Behavior conversations

Vaccinations

Personalized vaccine schedules based on age, health history, lifestyle, and local risk—not a blanket protocol.

  • Core vaccines
  • Lifestyle vaccines
  • Travel planning
  • Vaccine records

Dental Care

Complete oral care to protect comfort, appetite, and whole-body health.

  • Oral assessment
  • Digital dental imaging
  • Professional cleaning
  • Oral surgery

Diagnostics

Timely information helps us make sound decisions with less uncertainty.

  • In-house laboratory
  • Digital radiography
  • Ultrasound referral coordination
  • Specialty testing

Surgery

Attentive planning, monitoring, pain control, and recovery support before, during, and after a procedure.

  • Spay and neuter
  • Soft-tissue surgery
  • Pre-anesthetic testing
  • Recovery plans

Life-stage Care

Purposeful care for the needs that emerge at the beginning and later chapters of life.

  • Puppy and kitten visits
  • Senior screening
  • Mobility support
  • Quality-of-life guidance

Urgent & Sick Visits

Same-day availability may be offered for established patients based on clinical need and capacity. Call before coming.

  • Vomiting or diarrhea
  • Ear and skin concerns
  • Minor wounds
  • Sudden behavior changes
