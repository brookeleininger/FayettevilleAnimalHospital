Finding us

A familiar stop, close to home.

Our fictional location is imagined just south of downtown Fayetteville, with easy access from central Fayetteville and surrounding Northwest Arkansas communities.

Free on-site parking and a covered entry would make arrivals easier in every season.

Could this be an emergency?

If your pet is struggling to breathe, has collapsed, experienced major trauma, ingested a toxin, is bleeding heavily, or is having a prolonged seizure, contact the nearest open emergency veterinary facility immediately. Fayetteville Animal Hospital is fictional and cannot provide medical care.
