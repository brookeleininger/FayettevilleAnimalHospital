Dr. Theo Bennett

Meet Dr. Theo Bennett, a fictional veterinarian profile created for the Fayetteville Animal Hospital demonstration site.

A thoughtful approach to care

Theo is drawn to complex puzzles and unhurried conversations. His fictional practice style centers on interpreting trends, explaining choices plainly, and protecting comfort for aging pets.

Like every identity on this demonstration website, this profile is fictional and does not represent a real veterinary professional.

How Dr. works with families

Good plans begin with listening. Appointments make room for observations from home, clear explanations of findings, and questions about what is realistic for the pet and family.
