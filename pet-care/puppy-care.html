Puppy Care

A practical guide to puppy care from Fayetteville Animal Hospital's fictional veterinary team.

Care for this chapter

Puppy care combines a series of exams with vaccines, parasite prevention, nutrition, social development, and positive handling.

Build a healthy foundation during your puppy's fast-changing first year. The most useful plan changes as your pet grows, and continuity helps small observations become meaningful.

What we pay attention to

  • Changes in weight, appetite, thirst, sleep, and activity
  • Dental health, mobility, skin, coat, and behavior
  • Vaccination and parasite risks shaped by daily life
  • Questions about nutrition, enrichment, and home routines

Planning the next visit

Bring prior records, a list of foods and preventives, and your questions. We will map the next several visits so the plan feels predictable.
