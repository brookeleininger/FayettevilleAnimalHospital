New Patients

Everything new clients need to begin care with Fayetteville Animal Hospital.

Welcome to our fictional practice

Before the first visit, gather prior medical records, current medication and prevention details, food information, and a list of questions. Sending records ahead gives the clinical team time to understand the history.

Arrive a few minutes early and keep cats in secure carriers and dogs on non-retractable leashes. Tell us before arrival if your pet needs extra space or has had a difficult visit elsewhere.

Questions are welcome

Clear expectations make care easier. Contact the hospital for help choosing the right appointment type, preparing records, or understanding the next step. This site is a fictional development environment and cannot provide medical services.
